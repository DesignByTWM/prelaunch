### Why the legacy question matters, for Henry

This is a decision about trust, not sentiment, and it is worth explaining rather than just asking.

Search engines and AI assistants both evaluate a business on **E-E-A-T**: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness and Trustworthiness. It is the framework Google publishes for how quality gets assessed, and AI answer engines lean on the same signals when deciding which business to name in a response.

A new domain with no history is thin on all four. Right now, an AI assistant asked "who should I trust for custom wheels in Houston" has almost nothing to work with beyond a service list and an address, which is the same thing every competitor has.

The Tire and Wheel Master lineage changes that in a specific way:

- **Experience.** The work did not start last year. It came out of an established wheel and tire retailer running three showrooms in Houston, which is verifiable and long-standing.
- **Expertise.** It explains why fitment is treated as a measurement problem solved before ordering. That is a claim with a reason behind it rather than an adjective.
- **Authoritativeness.** A real, findable Houston business with its own footprint, reviews and history sits behind the story. AI engines can corroborate it independently, which is exactly what makes a claim usable to them.
- **Trustworthiness.** "A family that has been in the Houston wheel business for years opened a house dedicated to complete builds" is a story a buyer understands and believes. "We are passionate about quality" is not.

**The trade-off to weigh:** it is his father's business, and Henry may prefer Design By TWM to stand entirely on its own without leaning on it. That is a legitimate call and the site works without it. But the two businesses are operationally separate, the site says so, and mentioning the lineage takes nothing away from the independence of the house.

**If yes:** the story also becomes the spine of The House page, not just an FAQ answer, and the connection should be reinforced consistently everywhere so search engines resolve it as one coherent narrative.

**If no:** all four answers come out in a few minutes, and items 11.2 through 11.6 become considerably more urgent, because without the lineage there is very little else establishing experience.

## 14. REVIEWS AND SOCIAL PROOF PROGRAM

**This is the weakest part of the project and it is the one part Jose cannot solve technically.** Reviews have to be earned from real customers by the people who serve them. Liz and Henry own this workstream.

### Why this is urgent

There is currently no social proof anywhere on the site. No reviews, no testimonials, no ratings, no customer names. Every competitor analysis run on this market says the same thing: in luxury automotive customization, buyers evaluate trust long before they evaluate price. A site with beautiful photography and zero proof loses to an uglier site with 200 reviews.

It matters in three separate systems at once:

1. **Google local rankings.** Review count, review velocity and the keywords inside reviews are among the strongest ranking factors for the local map pack. This is the single highest-leverage thing that can be done for the 22 location pages.
2. **AI answer engines.** When someone asks an assistant to recommend a shop, the model weighs corroborating third-party evidence heavily. A business with no review footprint is very hard for a model to recommend with confidence.
3. **Conversion.** Proof on the page directly affects whether a visitor fills in the form.

| 14.12 | Permission to publish customer vehicles | Henry | Also covers Featured Builds, section 8 |

### A technical honesty note

Google prohibits a business from marking up its own collected reviews as `AggregateRating` schema on its own site. Sites that do it risk a manual action. So the plan is:

- Real reviews are **displayed** on the site as testimonials, which is entirely legitimate
- Aggregate rating markup is **not** self-generated
- The rating that appears in search results comes from the Google Business Profile, which is why 14.1 and 14.2 are the top of this list

There is no shortcut here and no way to fake it. The rankings follow the real reviews.

### Setting a target

Consistency beats volume. A sudden burst of reviews in one week looks manufactured and can get filtered, while a steady flow sustained over months is what actually moves the map pack. The right number is whatever the team can genuinely maintain every week without it becoming a chore that gets dropped. Henry and Liz to set that based on delivery volume and what the team can realistically keep up.

## 20. LOCATION PAGES · WHY THEY SAY COMING SOON

All 22 city pages exist and every footer link works. Each one currently shows the city name and a coming soon message. **This is intentional and it is the right call.**

### Why they are not built yet

The location program is the largest piece of the four-month search strategy. Twenty-two pages targeting twenty-two versions of the same commercial searches is how a business owns a metro area in local search.

It is also the easiest part of an SEO program to get badly wrong. Google specifically penalises what it calls doorway pages: sets of near-identical pages that exist to capture city names rather than to serve a reader. The penalty does not just affect those pages, it attaches to the domain.

The only way to build 22 pages that are genuinely distinct is to write 22 genuinely different pages. That means different content, different emphasis, different local context and different questions answered on each one. **That work has to be built on final, approved copy.** Writing it against copy that is still under review would mean rewriting all 22 the moment something changes on a service page.

### What is in place now

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| All 22 routes live and linked from the footer | DONE |
| Locations hub page listing all 22 | DONE |
| Each city page carries navigation, footer, city name and calls to action | DONE |
| **All 22 set to noindex** | DONE, deliberately |

The noindex matters. Publishing 22 thin pages now would attach a doorway page signal to the domain before the real program ever launches. Indexing gets switched on city by city as real content is written. Turning it on is one line of code. Undoing a doorway penalty is not.

### What unblocks it

Approved copy on the homepage, the ten service pages and the FAQ. Once those are locked, the location program runs against final content and the 22 pages get built properly, starting with Houston as the master.
